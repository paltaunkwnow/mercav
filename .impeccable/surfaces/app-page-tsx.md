# Sitio público de Mercav (mercav.lat)

## Scope and mode
Persuade. Landing (`app/page.tsx`, `components/home/*`), public header and footer (site mode), `/nosotros`, `/pitch`, `/inversores` and the legal pages (Read mode inside the same world). Animated icons reach the whole product.

## Audience, job, action
Owners of small and medium businesses across Latin America. Secondary: the Kosher community and companies that care about exposure (Pwn). Action: create an account, or open the app when signed in. Proof allowed:
- the three cofounders
- live computations from the real engines (calendar, payroll, Shabbat times)
- real public data Mercav stores (CISA KEV entries, published Kosher places)
- the real loyalty card component

No customers, testimonials or usage metrics.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The cartel, a Latin American hand-cut announcement poster, now carries a product-led landing. The app is the hero, surrounded by real stickers, and Business, Pwn and Kosher stand as three equal pillars. The user steered this on 2026-10-08, after v1 felt simple and repetitive, with ugly.cash, takenos.com and cocoalatam.com as guides. The page refuses both the repeated poster stack of v1 and the generic SaaS hero.
OWN-WORLD: Night or poster-white ground, four fluorescent inks, gold for the brand. Heavy condensed caps and system body type.
- Rounded sticker tiles and device frames now join the square poster bands.
- Each pillar has its own world: Business yellow, Pwn night with phosphor green, Kosher tejelet navy with gold.
- Mervi, in pixel art, is the living character.
STORY: The owner sees the app on a phone with real stickers around it (loyalty card, payslip, Pwn check, Shabbat time). They understand there are three apps under one account, try each pillar's working demo, see the countries, meet Mervi, read the founders and the FAQ, and create an account.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A centered two-line headline with the yellow line, the lead and the primary action. Below them, a phone with the app home screen, four tilted stickers that drift outward on scroll, and Mervi waving beside the phone. The phone overlaps the moving magenta band below.
FORM: Chicha poster and shop signage. It was position 4 of 7 on the original list (seed key 44fe7e12), and the composition was revised by the user to a product-led layout with a bento and pillars. Signature interactions:
- the sticky pillar bar that tracks Business, Pwn and Kosher
- stickers drifting on scroll
- the Pwn scan revealing source by source
- the country picker on the pixel map
- the Shabbat city picker
Raises kept from the declined hand:
- one composition per tile
- ink budget by state
- one truth per module
- a strict tonal ramp
- one label grid
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
